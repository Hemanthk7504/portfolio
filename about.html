// My Story

From Code to Intelligent Systems

I started my journey as a software engineer focused on backend systems — building REST APIs, designing databases, and learning the discipline of writing code that other people depend on. That foundation became the bedrock for everything that followed.

My pivot to AI wasn't a sudden switch — it was a gradual deepening. I started integrating ML models into backend services, then building ML pipelines, then designing entire ML platforms. Each project revealed new layers of complexity: serving latency, model drift, data quality, governance. I became obsessed with solving these production-layer problems.

Today I work at the intersection of AI research and software engineering. I architect GenAI systems that go beyond demos — systems with proper observability, evaluation pipelines, fallback mechanisms, and security guardrails. I believe the hardest part of AI engineering isn't the models — it's the infrastructure around them.

Outside of building, I'm a Technical Trainer for the Government of Andhra Pradesh's Naipunyam Initiative, teaching Python, ML, and software engineering to students and professionals. Teaching forces clarity, and clarity sharpens everything.

// What I Believe

Engineering Principles

🏗️

Production Over Demo

A system that impresses in a notebook but fails at 100 requests/second isn't ready. I design for production from day one — rate limits, error handling, observability, fallbacks.

🔍

Observability First

You can't fix what you can't see. I instrument every system I build — logs, metrics, traces, and ML-specific signals like drift, latency distribution, and confidence degradation.

🔐

Security by Design

Auth isn't an afterthought. RBAC, JWT validation, input sanitization, and rate limiting are built into the architecture from the start, not bolted on afterward.

📐

Clean Abstractions

Good systems have layers that are easy to reason about independently. I invest time in API design, module boundaries, and documentation that make future changes safe and fast.

⚡

Performance Matters

Milliseconds compound into seconds at scale. Redis caching, async processing, query optimization, and batch inference are not premature optimizations — they are professional standards.

🧪

Evaluate Everything

GenAI systems need evaluation pipelines — not just offline benchmarks but online A/B tests, faithfulness scoring, and human feedback loops. I treat evals as a first-class engineering concern.
